Walk around. The closer you get, the hotter it gets.

Open this page from an https:// link in Chrome on Android. Opening the file directly (file://) or using Firefox, Samsung Internet or iPhone Safari won't work.

Live signal tracking is turned off in Chrome.

  1. Type chrome://flags in the address bar.
  2. Search for experimental web platform.
  3. Set Experimental Web Platform features to Enabled.
  4. Tap Relaunch, then reopen this page.

Backup trick: Chrome's device picker (the Pick device button) shows signal bars next to each device. Reopen it as you move for a rough hot/cold.

Before you start

  1. Enable Experimental Web Platform features in chrome://flags and relaunch Chrome.
  2. Turn on the phone's Bluetooth and Location.
  3. Close or disconnect the walking pad's app on every phone. Most pads stop broadcasting while connected.
  4. Tap Pick device and choose the pad. Look for names like WalkingPad, KS-…, FS-… or Treadmill. Not sure? Watch which entry's signal bars change as you move.

Search tips

  • Walk slowly and pause 2–3 seconds in each spot. The reading needs a moment to settle.
  • Your body blocks signal, so turn around slowly in place to find the strongest direction.
  • Walls and furniture weaken the signal. A sudden jump usually means you've entered the right room.
  • Check under beds and sofas, behind doors, and in closets. Walking pads slide easily into flat spaces.
  • Roughly: −90 dBm or weaker is far or behind walls, −70 is the same room, −50 or stronger is within a metre or two.
